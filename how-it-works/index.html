Questions

Answered plainly

Is this the genuine fragrance, or a copy?

Genuine. Decanted from sealed retail bottles into clean glass atomisers. Nothing is added or diluted. If you have ever bought a sample from a department store counter, this is the same idea with a better sprayer.

Why is it cheaper per millilitre than a full bottle?

You are not paying for the bottle, the box, or the shelf space a 100ml takes up for two years. The liquid is the same; the packaging is not.

How long does it last on skin?

Between two and eight hours depending on the scent and your skin. Citrus goes first, amber and oud go last. Spraying on moisturised skin helps.

Can I get one that is not listed?

Ask. If it is available from our supplier in a full bottle, we can usually decant it.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Add a note at checkout and we will leave the prices off the packing slip. Nothing on the outside says what is inside.

Do you ship outside New Zealand?

Not yet. Fragrance is classed as a dangerous good for air freight, which makes international post more trouble than it is worth at this size.
